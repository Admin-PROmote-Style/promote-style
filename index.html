Meet your Bot Office

Four helpers that take routine work off your plate, so you can focus on running your business.

Social Bot

Never stare at a blank post again.

Your Social Bot is your creative partner for social media. It helps you come up with ideas, write posts, and keep a steady schedule.

  • Brainstorms post ideas around your products, events, promotions, and seasons
  • Writes captions in your brand's voice, ready to use or tweak
  • Turns one idea into versions for different platforms
  • Suggests hashtags, timing, and calls to action
  • Plans a content calendar so you're never scrambling
  • Posts to X automatically on your schedule, once you approve

You get: Fresh, on-brand posts every week without the writer's block.

Reviews Bot

Every review gets a thoughtful reply.

Reviews shape how new customers see you. Your Reviews Bot makes sure no one is ignored.

  • Drafts warm thank-yous for happy customers
  • Writes calm, professional responses to complaints
  • Flags reviews that need your personal attention
  • Spots patterns in what customers praise or complain about

You get: A reputation that shows you care, in minutes instead of hours.

Inbox Bot

Nothing important slips through.

Your Inbox Bot reads through the noise and surfaces what matters.

  • Sorts customer emails, inquiries, and orders from the clutter
  • Drafts clear replies for you to review and send
  • Highlights time-sensitive messages first
  • Summarizes your inbox so you can catch up fast

You get: Faster responses and fewer missed opportunities.

Website Bot

Update your website by just asking.

Powered by Client Style, your Website Bot keeps your site current without a developer.

  • Updates hours, prices, specials, and announcements in plain words
  • Adds new photos, products, and pages
  • Keeps your information consistent and accurate
  • Every change waits for your approval before going live

You get: A website that's always up to date, without the hassle.

You stay in control. Your bots draft, suggest, and prepare; nothing goes public without your OK.

Not from us. You only pay for your own Cursor plan.
